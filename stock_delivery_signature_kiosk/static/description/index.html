Stock Delivery Signature Kiosk

Send Odoo delivery signatures to a tablet-friendly kiosk

This module adds a tablet kiosk flow for outgoing deliveries. Instead of rotating a desktop screen or asking the customer to sign on the back-office interface, warehouse staff can send a delivery to a dedicated kiosk page and collect the signature on a tablet.

Key Features

  • Adds a Sign on Tablet action on delivery orders
  • Provides a dedicated kiosk URL in Inventory settings
  • Queues signature requests when several deliveries are waiting
  • Shows customer, origin, schedule, and delivery lines on the kiosk
  • Saves the signature back to the Odoo delivery document
  • Stores the signer name and the tablet signing timestamp

How It Works

  1. Open Inventory settings and copy the kiosk URL to the tablet browser.
  2. Open an outgoing delivery in Odoo.
  3. Click Sign on Tablet.
  4. The delivery appears on the tablet kiosk.
  5. The customer signs on the tablet and the delivery is updated in Odoo.

Notes

  • This module extends the standard Odoo delivery signature flow.
  • Tablet signing is only available for outgoing deliveries.
  • The kiosk link is company-specific.
